# Business Requirements

## Project Overview

Knapp Healthcare is a regional healthcare organization with approximately 2,500 employees operating across hospitals, outpatient clinics, and administrative offices.

Following several data protection incidents involving sensitive business and patient information, executive leadership has approved the implementation of Microsoft Purview to strengthen information governance, reduce data loss, and improve regulatory compliance.

---

## Current Challenges

The organization has identified the following business risks:

- Employee payroll information has been accidentally shared with external recipients.
- Patient records have been uploaded to unauthorized cloud storage services.
- Financial reports are being shared without encryption or classification.
- Clinical research documents are inconsistently protected across departments.
- Users are unsure how to properly classify sensitive information.
- Security teams lack visibility into document activity and policy violations.

---

## Business Objectives

The Microsoft Purview implementation must:

- Protect sensitive healthcare and financial information.
- Reduce accidental data loss.
- Classify business data consistently.
- Automate information protection where possible.
- Improve audit visibility and investigation capabilities.
- Support regulatory compliance and organizational security policies.

---

## Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful when:

- Sensitive documents are classified using Sensitivity Labels.
- Data Loss Prevention (DLP) policies reduce the risk of accidental data exposure.
- Auto-labeling protects sensitive documents automatically.
- Security analysts can investigate incidents using Microsoft Purview Audit and KQL.
- Employees follow a consistent information classification process.
