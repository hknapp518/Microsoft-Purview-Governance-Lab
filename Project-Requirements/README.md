# Project Requirements

## Overview

This section documents the initial planning and assessment phase of the Microsoft Purview implementation for Knapp Healthcare.

Before implementing Microsoft Purview, it was necessary to understand the organization's business operations, identify sensitive data, and define the security and compliance objectives that would guide the deployment.

The documents in this section establish the foundation for the remainder of the project by identifying business risks, regulatory considerations, and the types of information requiring protection.

---

## Objectives

The primary objectives of this phase were to:

- Understand Knapp Healthcare's business environment.
- Identify sensitive business and healthcare data.
- Document regulatory and compliance requirements.
- Define business drivers for implementing Microsoft Purview.
- Establish the scope for information classification and protection.

---

## Contents

### Business Requirements

Defines the organization's business goals, security challenges, compliance requirements, and information governance objectives.

### Data Inventory

Identifies the organization's critical data assets, their business owners, storage locations, and sensitivity levels to support Microsoft Purview classification and protection strategies.

---

## Outcome

Completing the project requirements phase ensured that all Microsoft Purview configurations aligned with business objectives rather than technical assumptions, providing a structured foundation for information classification, protection, governance, and data loss prevention.
