# Sensitivity Label Taxonomy

## Purpose

As part of the Microsoft Purview implementation, Knapp Healthcare established a sensitivity label taxonomy to ensure consistent classification and protection of organizational information.

The taxonomy aligns business data with Microsoft Purview Information Protection and supports encryption, access control, Data Loss Prevention (DLP), and compliance initiatives.

---

## Sensitivity Labels

| Sensitivity Label | Business Purpose | Example Data |
|-------------------|------------------|--------------|
| Public | Information approved for unrestricted public distribution. | Marketing materials, public website content |
| Information approved for public release | Business information reviewed and approved for external sharing. | Press releases, public announcements |
| Internal information | Information intended for employees and authorized internal users only. | Employee handbook, internal procedures |
| Personal (PII) | Personally Identifiable Information requiring privacy protection. | Employee records, Social Security Numbers, dates of birth |
| Confidential – Financial Data | Sensitive financial information requiring restricted access. | Payroll reports, budgets, financial statements |
| Confidential – PHI Data | Protected Health Information (PHI) subject to healthcare privacy requirements. | Patient records, diagnoses, treatment plans |
| Highly Confidential – PCI Data | Payment Card Industry (PCI) data requiring the highest level of protection. | Credit card numbers, payment transactions |

---

## Classification Principles

Knapp Healthcare classifies information based on:

- Business impact
- Regulatory requirements
- Data sensitivity
- Risk of unauthorized disclosure
- Operational necessity

Each document is assigned the most appropriate Microsoft Purview sensitivity label to ensure consistent protection across Microsoft 365 services.

---

## Business Value

The sensitivity label taxonomy enables Knapp Healthcare to:

- Standardize information classification
- Protect sensitive healthcare and financial information
- Support Data Loss Prevention (DLP) policies
- Enable automatic document classification
- Improve regulatory compliance
- Reduce the risk of accidental data exposure
