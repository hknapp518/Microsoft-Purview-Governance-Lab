# Information Protection

## Overview

This section documents the implementation of Microsoft Purview Information Protection for Knapp Healthcare.

Based on the organization's information classification strategy, sensitivity labels and publishing policies were created to protect sensitive healthcare, financial, and personally identifiable information. These controls help ensure that business-critical data is appropriately classified and protected throughout its lifecycle.

---

## Objectives

The primary objectives of this phase were to:

- Implement Microsoft Purview sensitivity labels.
- Configure label publishing policies for end users.
- Protect healthcare, financial, and personally identifiable information.
- Support regulatory compliance through consistent data protection.
- Establish a scalable information protection framework.

## Contents

### Sensitivity Labels

Documents the sensitivity labels created within Microsoft Purview, including Public, Internal Information, Personal (PII), Confidential – Financial Data, Confidential – PHI Data, and Highly Confidential – PCI Data.

### Label Publishing Policy

Describes the publishing policy used to make sensitivity labels available to users while enforcing organizational requirements such as justification when removing or lowering a sensitivity label.

### Auto-Labeling Policy

Documents the auto-labeling policy configured in simulation mode to identify Protected Health Information (PHI) across Exchange Online, SharePoint Online, and OneDrive using Microsoft Purview's built-in sensitive information types.

---

## Business Value

By implementing Microsoft Purview Information Protection, Knapp Healthcare establishes a consistent approach to classifying and protecting sensitive information. These controls reduce the risk of accidental data exposure, strengthen regulatory compliance, and enable users to apply appropriate protection to business-critical information.
